Data is at the heart of everything we do—from modeling and strategy development to production monitoring and compliance. As we expand our data-driven investment initiatives, we are seeking a Technical Lead in Data Science to guide the design, implementation, and evolution of our analytical systems and mentor a growing team of data scientists. You will collaborate with stakeholders in engineering, research, and leadership while driving innovation and excellence in our data science practice.
