operations.Job LocationThis is a hybrid role based at our Corporate Office in Atlanta, GA.Job Responsibilities Design, implement, and support standardized integration between OT systems (PLC, SCADA, integration standards, patterns, and approved technologies.Support plant initiatives requiring data exchange between production systems and enterprise applications.Partner with IT infrastructure teams monitoring.Support secure remote access solutions for vendors and plant support teams.Implement and maintain OT cybersecurity controls in alignment with IT security policies and industry standards (IEC 62443, NIST).Participate in OT risk assessments, vulnerability remediation, and audit activities.Ensure changes to OT environments follow IT change management and approval processes.Provide
