Posting: "Senior System Engineer at Apex Technology Corporation"

About the job ***Summary***The Senior Systems Engineer is Apex’s highest-level technical resource — a deeply experienced engineer responsible for designing, scoping, and delivering complex IT infrastructure projects for managed services clients. This role leads project engineering from design through implementation, writes technical scopes, and serves as the senior technical authority across Microsoft, networking, and systems environments. While project work is the primary focus, the Senior Systems Engineer also serves as an escalation resource for the most complex issues the service desk team cannot resolve. Beyond individual technical contributions, this role is expected to mentor and develop the engineers and technicians around them, raising the technical standard of the entire team. ***Primary
