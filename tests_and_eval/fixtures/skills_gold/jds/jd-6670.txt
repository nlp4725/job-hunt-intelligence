The Role The Strategic Partnership Manager will report directly to our SVP of Frontier Lab Safety, who owns the overall relationships and technical scope across our AI lab customer base. As we grow our lab engagements rapidly, we need a dedicated operator to make sure everything around those relationships runs cleanly. You function as the connective tissue across customers, internal teams, and leadership so nothing falls through the cracks. This person is an incredible project manager, strategic partner and tactical driver functioning between sales operations, customer success, and engineering with a strong emphasis on execution and follow through. What You’ll Do: Support the SVP of Frontier Lab Safety Act as the operational backbone for all lab relationships, owning the workflows and coordination to ensure services can scale. Translate technical scopes into clear milestones and deliverables for broader team execution. Coordinate initial calls with new customers, including scheduling, prep materials, and post-call follow-up. Monitor dedicated Slack channels for lab customers and surface new signals or requests to internal teams quickly. Ensure that internal teams can focus on high-leverage work by owning the execution details of every engagement. Deal Execution and Pipeline Management Own the CRM for all lab relationships capturing every contact, conversation, and opportunity to reflect reality. Drive deals to close in real time, working with procurement and legal teams to keep contracts moving. Identify and resolve blockers before they impact timelines, maintaining full visibility into contract status. Maintain clear visibility into pipeline status and flag any delays before they become problems. Milestone and Delivery Coordination Keep the accounting team informed of milestone status and flag when deliverables are ready for invoicing. Align internal teams and customer expectations on timelines, tracking progress and flagging risks early. Provide light project management across active engagements to keep expectations aligned. Be a consistent, reliable point of contact for lab partners on operational and logistical questions. Who You Are: 3+ years in partnerships, account management, customer success, or sales operations, ideally in a technical environment.
