About the job

About The Role

Join our intelligence team to build predictive models that reduce patient no-shows and automate clinical workflows. You will lead the development of our core AI assistant features.

What You'll Do

  • Develop and deploy machine learning models for predictive scheduling and patient triage.
  • Build NLP pipelines to extract structured data from unstructured clinical notes.
  • Optimize model inference times for real-time applications within the EMR.
  • Collaborate with backend engineers to integrate ML models into production services.
  • Continuously monitor model performance and retrain on new data streams.

Requirements
  • Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or equivalent experience.
  • Strong proficiency in Python and popular ML frameworks (PyTorch, TensorFlow, Scikit-learn).
  • Experience deploying ML models into production environments.
  • Understanding of NLP techniques, Large Language Models, and prompt engineering.
  • Experience handling sensitive data (familiarity with HIPAA/GDPR constraints is a big plus).
