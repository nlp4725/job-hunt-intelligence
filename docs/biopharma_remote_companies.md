## Methodology

- Company list pulled from biopharmguy.com's software-company directory, filtered to entries with at least one US state location (or "Virtual").
- Researched in 4 batches (75/70/70/69 companies) via parallel background agents doing web search + career-page lookups, plus direct curl/grep career-page checks for a 20-company gap where one batch exhausted its session web-search budget.
- **Remote**: explicit remote-first policy or multiple current remote-tagged job postings found.
- **Hybrid**: explicit mix of remote/office language, or some but not all roles remote.
- **On-site**: explicit on-site-only / in-office-required language found.
- **Unknown**: no current job postings found, no careers page found, or no explicit remote/onsite signal on the page checked. This is the largest bucket and is mostly small (<50-employee) niche startups with minimal web presence — not evidence they're on-site, just unresolved. Would need direct outreach or LinkedIn research to resolve further.
- This is a snapshot from live web research on 2026-07-30 — career pages and policies change; verify directly before treating any single row as current truth.
